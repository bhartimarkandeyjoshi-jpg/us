My love,

Two years.


Two years sounds like a number. But when I think about everything hidden inside those two years, it feels like an entire universe that belongs only to us.


There were laughs. Stupid conversations. Random little moments. Hugs that lasted longer than they needed to. And days when simply having you beside me made everything feel okay.


Somewhere along the way, you stopped being just someone I loved and became the person I look for in every beautiful moment.


I don't know exactly what our future will look like.


But whenever I imagine the beautiful parts of it... somehow you're there.


Thank you for holding my hand. Thank you for holding me. Thank you for making me laugh. Thank you for being my person.


And if I had to go back to the beginning, knowing everything I know now...


I would still choose you. Again. And again. And again.

Forever yours ❤️
